# product-ad: editorial rules

- Cut strategy `visual`: run `nve shots`, LOOK at every contact sheet, and pick the moments yourself. The draft picks are only a starting point.
- Prefer: hero close-ups, the product in hand, pouring/opening/action, texture, packaging with the logo readable. Drop blurry, dark, shaky or repetitive shots.
- Order: hook (most striking action) -> product reveal -> benefits (one stamp each) -> packaging/CTA.
- Shots 1.2–3 s; change something every 2–3 s. Action shots may run longer if the motion is the point.
- Text stamps carry the message (no voice): 2–5 words each, one per benefit. Captions only if there is speech.
- Music drives the edit: without speech, bgm is the main audio (volume 0.5–0.8).
